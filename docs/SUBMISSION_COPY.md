# POSE × Vouch — Submission Copy

## One-line pitch

**POSE is a mobile-first social puzzle where humans are the game pieces — then Vouch captures who they would choose to play with again.**

## Short description

Two to eight Decentraland players enter a tiny arena. Every round shows a new formation and a set of glowing pads. The group has seconds to physically move their avatars into the correct positions and hold the pose together. Correct positions turn green in real time. With one visitor, the scene offers a short rehearsal; with two or more, the real cooperative game starts automatically.

## Why it belongs in Friendzone

POSE does not bolt social features onto a single-player game: another human is part of the puzzle itself. It uses only movement, making the interaction naturally mobile-first, easy to understand, and performance-friendly. Short synchronized rounds create immediate retries, while changing formations make groups communicate, improvise, laugh, and produce memorable avatar-photo moments.

## Key points for judges

- Designed around the existing Decentraland mobile movement controls.
- Meaningful social interaction is the core mechanic.
- Works as a persistent standalone scene; no host or scheduled event required.
- No external backend required for the MVP.
- Primitive-only scene minimizes loading and rendering cost.
- Open source under MIT.
- 2–8 active players; safe solo rehearsal for first-time/judge testing.

## Memorable line

> **Most multiplayer games put humans inside the game. POSE makes the humans the game.**

## The Vouch twist

Solving a formation unlocks a five-second Vouch Round. Players physically stand beside the teammate they recommend. It turns an abstract “like” into a recommendation grounded in a real cooperative interaction.

**POSE creates the interaction. Vouch captures the trust signal.**

For this competition build, vouches are deliberately session-local: no token, wallet transaction, paid backend, or permanent reputation score is required to judge the mechanic.
